Community Guidelines

At LinkedUp, we are building a premium professional network where meaningful connections happen. These guidelines ensure our community remains a safe, respectful, and high-integrity environment for everyone.

verified Verified Only
photo_camera

Authentic Photos

Transparency is our bedrock. We require current, clear, and authentic photos of yourself. Profiles using AI-generated avatars, landscape shots as profile pics, or misleading filters will be removed. Your face should be clearly visible.

  • check_circle No group photos as primary images
  • check_circle No heavy obscuring sunglasses
  • check_circle Professional & respectful attire
handshake

Respectful Behavior

Treat every connection with the same professional courtesy you would show in a boardroom. Communication should be consensual, polite, and meaningful.

"Professionalism is not the job you do, it's how you do the job."
gavel

Zero Tolerance

We maintain a zero-tolerance policy for hate speech, harassment, bullying, or any form of discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or orientation.

Immediate account termination for violators.
security

Integrity First

LinkedUp is for networking and dating, not solicitation. Any form of spam, multi-level marketing (MLM), or commercial promotion without prior authorization is strictly prohibited. Protecting our users from financial scams is our top priority.

No Solicitation No MLMs No Scams
shield_person

Reporting & Enforcement

Our moderation team reviews every report within 24 hours. Violations of these guidelines can result in warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans depending on the severity and frequency of the infraction.

Our Promise to You

LinkedUp is more than an app; it's a community built on trust. We promise to enforce these rules fairly and consistently to maintain the premium experience you expect and deserve.

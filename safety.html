Essential Safety Tips

groups

Meet in Public

Always arrange your first few meetings in busy, public locations like coffee shops or restaurants. Never meet at a private residence or isolated area.

block

Block & Move On

You have total control. If someone makes you feel uncomfortable or harassed, use the block feature immediately. They will not be notified.

share_location

Share Your Plans

Tell a friend or family member where you're going and who you're meeting. Send them a screenshot of the person's profile as a precaution.

payments

Financial Safety

Never send money or share bank details with anyone you meet online. MATCHEDIN will never ask for your password via message.

no_photography

Protect Identity

Keep your personal contact information (email, phone, address) private until you have built significant trust in person.

psychology

Trust Your Gut

If a conversation or profile feels suspicious or "too good to be true," it probably is. Don't hesitate to end the interaction.
